## 💬 About Me

I'm an Electrical Engineering student interested in hardware and PCB design, especially mixed-signal circuits, digital hardware, and high-speed design.

I work on projects involving analog circuits, power supplies, embedded systems, and multilayer PCBs. I enjoy working on both hardware design and hands-on testing.

- 📌**Currently learning:** High-speed interface layout, DSP, and SoC hardware design.
- 🚩**Goal:** Become a well-rounded hardware engineer and PCB designer.
- ☕**Powered by:** Coffee.
<details>
<summary><h3>⚙️ Technical Skills</h3></summary>

  * **PCB Design:** 4–10 layer PCBs, stackup planning, controlled impedance, differential pairs, length matching, DFM
  * **Analog & Mixed-Signal:** Op-amps, active filters, audio preamps, headphone amplifiers
  * **Digital Hardware:** Digital circuits, basic SoC hardware design
  * **Power Electronics:** Switching and linear regulators, synchronous buck converters
  - **Hardware Development:** Component selection, datasheet analysis, simulation, schematic design, PCB layout, debugging
  - **Embedded Systems:** STM32, LL drivers, real-time control
  - **Control Systems:** Digital LQR, PI control, analog and digital feedback loops
  - **Testing & Measurement:** Oscilloscope, thermal camera, FFT analysis
  - **Engineering Tools:** KiCad, LTspice, MATLAB, STM32Cube
